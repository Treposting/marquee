Marquee is a free, open-source menu-bar app for Mac. It takes screenshots and screen recordings, and it remembers everything you copy.

Download for Mac Buy me a coffee Source on GitHub

Runs on macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel. MIT licensed.

New: Marquee for Windows preview, an early build for Windows 10 and 11.

Ten things it does, from one app

One shortcut opens All-in-One. Every other action has a button in the Marquee window, and a shortcut if you want one.

  • Choose

    One shortcut, one toolbar. Drag an area, or pick Full Screen or a window from the menu, then screenshot it, record it, copy it with ⌘C, read its text, pin it or pick a color.

    ⌥⇧⌘A

  • Capture

    Grab an area, a window or the whole screen, or a whole scrolling page in one long screenshot. In All-in-One, press ↩ for the selection, F for the full screen, W for a window or S to scroll. Drag again any time to change the area.

    ↩ · F · W · S

  • Record

    Record an area, the full screen or a single window to MP4 with your microphone, desktop audio, or both, and your camera in a bubble you can style and drag, all chosen on a toolbar right under the area. Start instantly or after a 3-2-1 countdown, and watch the time tick in the menu bar. Save any recording as a looping GIF.

    R · ⇧F · ⇧W

  • Mark up

    Add arrows, boxes, text, highlights and numbered steps. Pixelate anything private before you send it.

    from the overlay

  • Measure

    Point at anything to see the distance to the nearest edges, or drag around a button to snap to its exact size. Add guides to check alignment, on every display at once. Copy the numbers or capture them. Needs macOS 14.

    your shortcut

  • Pick

    Click any pixel on screen to copy its color as HEX, RGB, HSL or SwiftUI code. Every color you pick is kept in the Colors library.

    your shortcut

  • Read

    Select part of the screen and copy the text in it. Recognition runs on your Mac, with no upload.

    ⌥⇧⌘A, then T

  • Pin

    Float a screenshot above every window while you work. Scroll on it to fade it, double-click to close it.

    from the overlay

  • Keep

    Text, links, images and files you copy are saved with the app they came from, and they stay searchable.

    ⇧⌘V

  • Paste

    Pick an item from your history and it goes straight into the app you were typing in.

    ↩⌘1–9

Screenshots land here too

Copy a capture from the overlay and it joins your history, so last week's screenshot is still a search away.

Passwords are skipped

Password managers tag what they copy with org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType, and Marquee never records those items. Copies from Passwords, Keychain and popular password managers are skipped even untagged, and you can add any app to ignore.

Stays on your Mac

No account and no sync. Marquee only goes online to check for new versions and to load previews of links you copy, and you can turn both off. History lives in your Library folder.

Install

Takes about a minute, once. After that, Marquee updates itself: you get a prompt with the release notes, a restart installs the new version, and your permissions stay as they were.

  1. Download the DMG

    Get the latest release from GitHub, open it, and drag Marquee into Applications.

  2. Open it once with right-click

    Releases aren't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first launch: right-click the app and choose Open.

  3. Allow Screen Recording

    macOS asks the first time you capture. Accessibility is optional; it lets Marquee paste into other apps for you.

On Windows?

An early preview of Marquee for Windows is out: the tray app, shortcuts and settings for now, with screenshots, recording and clipboard history coming in the next previews. Run the installer; it isn't code-signed yet, so choose More info → Run anyway when Windows asks.

Free, open source, and yours to change.
